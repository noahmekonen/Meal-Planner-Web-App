Meal Planner

Welcome!

Meal Planner is built to help college students find meals, when they are either not sure on what they want to eat or just want a meal recommendation. You can choose below to start a meal or view your saved meals!

How the app works:

Step 1: Choose start a meal.

Step 2: Type your name in.

Step 3: Choose your meal type, protein, and budget

Step 4: Click on the "Check Results" button and your recommended meal will come up.

Step 5: Click on the history icon on the top in the navigation bar to view your meals history.

Step 6: Clear your history if you would like to.

Step 7: Click the back button on the navigation bar to go back a screen.
